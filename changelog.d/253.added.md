Added `terminaltexteffects.utils.geometry3d`, low-level 3D building blocks for effect authors: vectors, rotations, a camera that projects onto terminal cells with tall-cell correction, solid meshes (boxes, prisms, pyramids, regular solids, cylinders, spheres and tori), depth-buffered rasterization with near-plane clipping, wireframe edges with optional depth-tested hidden lines, and Lambert shading. `rasterize` and `wireframe` take `bounds=Bounds(...)` to fill only cells on the canvas.
