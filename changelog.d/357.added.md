Shapes can be sized to fit text: `Cylinder.fit_text(characters)`, or `fit(columns, rows)`, returns a shape that the text wraps once around with `surface.layout_text`, every character in its own cell. `Box`, `Cube`, `Prism`, `Cylinder`, `Sphere` and `Plane` support it, and settings such as a prism's `sides` pass through. A cube or sphere grows past the text's width when its sides couldn't otherwise hold the text.
