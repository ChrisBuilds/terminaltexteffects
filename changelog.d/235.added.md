`tte --export` writes an animated GIF or WebP of the canvas when the file ends in `.gif` or `.webp`, and shows
its progress on standard error. The animations are drawn with Pillow, installed on Python 3.10 or later with
`pip install 'terminaltexteffects[export]'`, and need no other programs. The `all` extra installs both
`export` and `tui`.
