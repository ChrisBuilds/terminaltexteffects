Drawn bodies can show text over a solid fill. `Solid(background=True)` paints its lit color as each cell's background, and `Textured(background=...)` fills every cell under the text with a color or a lit gradient. `Textured(ink=Ink(...))` sets how the text is colored: `Ink(color)` takes a color or gradient, `spread` picks whether a gradient follows the light or runs across the text (`Gradient.Direction`) so its colors stay on their letters, and `light` sets how much shadow darkens the text, from 0 (glowing) to 1. `Ink` is on `tte` beside the looks, and `Ink(gradient)` replaces the `gradient` argument `Textured` took before this release.
