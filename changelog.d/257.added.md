Added `Space3D` (`terminaltexteffects.engine.space3d`) for moving an effect's own characters through 3D. Bodies with shapes such as cubes, prisms and spheres turn, move and scale step by step or with queued tweens. Characters mount on anchors laid out on a body, ride it with their scenes still running, and are released back to ordinary paths. Mounting is part of `Motion` and adds the `MOUNT_COMPLETE` and `MOUNT_RELEASED` events, the `MOUNT` and `UNMOUNT` actions, and the `LIGHT` and `DEPTH` scene sync metrics.
