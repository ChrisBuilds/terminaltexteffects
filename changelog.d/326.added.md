Characters now have a 3D position. `Path.new_waypoint()` and `Motion.position` accept a `Vec3` as well as a `Coord`, and a height off the text plane moves a character toward the camera, projected through `terminal.world.camera`; nearer characters paint over farther ones in the same layer. Every terminal has a `world` with a `root`, a `text_plane`, a camera and a light (`world.set_light()`), and `Camera.set_perspective()` changes how strong the perspective is.
