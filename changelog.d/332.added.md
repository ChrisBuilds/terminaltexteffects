Bodies can be drawn and animated. `body.draw(look)` draws a body with characters the world adds: `Shaded` fills faces with a symbol ramp and gradient by their angle to the light, `Solid` uses one symbol, `Textured` wraps the input text (or any string) over the faces, with offset, repeat and scale controls, and `Wireframe` draws the edges. Looks can be set per face. Every drawn body shares one depth order with the characters, and a character facing the camera on a drawn body's surface draws over it. Bodies turn and scale now (`rotate`, with `local=True` for the body's own axes) or over frames with `spin`, `settle`, `turn_to`, `turn_to_rest`, `random_pose` and `scale_to`, with `hold`, `on_complete`, `on_group_complete`, `cancel` and `reset`. `world.camera` moves now or over frames (dolly, truck, pedestal, pan, tilt, roll, `look_at`, `orbit`, `zoom_to`), and `world.set_light(..., space="camera")` makes the light follow it. `character.view` reads a character's cell, depth, nearness, facing, light and face, and new `LIGHT` and `DEPTH` scene sync metrics follow it. A character behind the camera is hidden, as is one facing away on a body with `hide_facing_away`. `world.screen` holds characters on fixed canvas cells whatever the camera does, `world.bodies` lists the bodies, and `body.surface` raises for a body without one (check `body.has_surface`).
