The world now has everything `Space3D` had, and `Space3D`, mounts, anchors and their layouts are gone. `world.camera` moves now or over frames (dolly, truck, pedestal, pan, tilt, roll, `look_at`, `orbit`, `zoom_to`), and bodies turn and scale over frames with `spin`, `settle`, `turn_to`, `turn_to_rest`, `random_pose` and `scale_to`; tweens compose as deltas and play inside `update()`. `body.draw(look)` draws a body with characters the world adds, every drawn body sharing one depth resolve with the characters, and a character facing the camera on a drawn body's surface draws over it. `character.view` reads a character's cell, depth, nearness, facing, light and face, and LIGHT and DEPTH scenes follow it. A character behind the camera is hidden, as is one facing away on a body with `hide_facing_away`. `world.screen` holds characters on fixed canvas cells whatever the camera does, `world.bodies` lists the bodies made with `world.body()`, and `body.surface` raises for a body without one (check `body.has_surface`).
