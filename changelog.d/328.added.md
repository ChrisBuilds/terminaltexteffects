Bodies move like characters: `body.motion` takes paths of `Vec3` waypoints in the parent's frame and `body.event_handler` registers path events. Any character or body can `attach(parent, at=None)` to a body, keeping its place in the world unless `at` places it, and `detach()` into the world, with new `ATTACHED` and `DETACHED` events and `ATTACH` and `DETACH` actions. `world.remove(body)` leaves the body's children where they are. A waypoint can be a node, a live target that the path homes in on every tick.
