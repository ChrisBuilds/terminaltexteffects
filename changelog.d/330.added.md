Bodies can have a shape, `world.body(Cube(16))`, and shaped bodies have a `surface`: a grid of cells characters sit on and travel over, for planes, boxes, cubes, prisms, pyramids, cylinders, spheres and tori. Columns go around and rows run pole to pole, and positions are always normalized. A waypoint on a closed surface takes the shortest straight route over the folds, and `path.walk(columns, rows)` goes straight ahead by an amount, over lids and round laps. `surface.face(name)` gives a face as a grid of its own, with `at()`, `center`, `contains()`, `row_extent()` and `random_coord()`. `layout_text` on a face or a whole surface attaches characters as the input reads, extending past the edge or clipping. `surface.target()` is a live target that stays on its spot as the body moves. Shapes, including a flat `Plane`, are in `terminaltexteffects.engine.shapes`.
