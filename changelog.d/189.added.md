`--hold SECONDS` and `--alt-screen` also work for live effects, not only `--play`. `--hold` keeps the final frame of every playback or recording on screen, including the last one, before continuing or exiting, and `--alt-screen` runs output in the terminal's alternate screen and restores the original screen afterward. `--shuffle` still requires `--play`, and `--hold` and `--alt-screen` cannot be combined with `--export`.
