Characters on the surface of a body drawn with a background fill (`Solid(background=True)`, `Textured(background=...)`) are drawn over the fill instead of replacing the cell, so text placed with `surface.layout_text` or `attach` sits on a solid shape. The cell follows the character's symbol, colors and layer every frame and shows the fill again once the character leaves. `Solid(ink=Ink(...))` colors those characters: an `Ink` without a color keeps theirs, darkened by the light, and one with a color replaces it.
