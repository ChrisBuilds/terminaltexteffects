Fixed six `Space3D` and `geometry3d` defects found in review. Surfaces and wireframes only fill cells on the canvas, so a body close to the camera or far off screen no longer stalls a frame; `rasterize` and `wireframe` take `bounds=Bounds(...)` for this. Characters released from an `on_complete` callback are handed over at the final pose. `Camera.cell_to_world` round-trips at z=0 with any focal length. Mounted characters and surface cells are ordered by their exact depth, so the nearer one draws on top. Points from `surface_points` on a mesh no longer coincide. A wireframe vertex exactly at the camera no longer raises.
